New to DeFi?

Getting started is simple! You can buy $CTK tokens with your credit or debit card with two options:

Option 1: Check out using Apple Pay, Google Pay or simply enter your card details at checkout. You may need to complete verification or KYC depending on your jurisdiction, using our payment services provider. This is a really simple and efficient process.

Option 2: No verification or KYC required.

Step 1: Buy USDT Using Your Card

Head to one of these trusted platforms to purchase USDT on the Ethereum network.

  • Ramp Network
  • Transak
  • MoonPay

Follow the platform’s instructions to buy $USDT and send it directly to your wallet.Tip: To cover all costs, including gas fees for buying $CTK, we recommend purchasing at least $50 worth of USDT.

Step 2: Swap USDT for $CTK

Once USDT is in your wallet, use it to purchase $CTK.

  • Connect your wallet to the ConstructKoin website.
  • Follow the steps outlined in the “How to Buy ConstructKoin” ($CTK) Tutorial.

Note: Make sure your wallet has enough USDT to cover both the CTK purchase and any gas fees.
