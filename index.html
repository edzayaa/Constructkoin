    What is ConstructKoin (CTK)?

    ConstructKoin (CTK) is the world's first AI-powered, real estate-backed Web3 finance protocol. It bridges blockchain technology with tangible property assets. By staking CTK, investors participate directly in funding real estate development finance deals secured by UK-registered title deeds, while earning projected yields from a combination of streams, 8-12% APR, a share in project profits plus token appreciation through buybacks and burns

    Protocol Genesis & Off-Chain Foundation: The ConstructKoin protocol began as off-chain operations to establish robust security frameworks and proven revenue streams before blockchain integration. This strategic approach created a solid foundation of:

    • Established UK lending operations through ConstructKoin Limited, generating real cash flows and building loan book track records
    • Proven AI technology deployment in traditional property finance, demonstrating measurable efficiency gains
    • Regulatory compliance frameworks across multiple jurisdictions, ensuring seamless transition to on-chain operations
    • Partnership networks with leading UK property developers, creating sustainable deal flow and revenue generation

  • How is CTK different from other cryptocurrencies?

    Most tokens are speculative or meme-driven. ConstructKoin is utility-driven and asset-backed:

    • Real Assets: All lending is secured by UK property title deeds.
    • AI-Driven: Underwriting uses machine learning models for instant risk pricing (95% speed improvement over traditional 2-6 week processes).
    • Investor Yields: Token holders receive predictable returns from real borrower payments.
    • Deflationary Model: 20% of protocol fees are used to buy back and burn CTK, reducing supply.
    • Stablecoin Evolution: At £100M in collateralized loans, CTK transitions into the world's first AIpowered, real estate-backed stablecoin.

  • What happens if a borrower defaults on a loan?

    All CTK-funded loans are secured by a legal charge registered on the title deed with HM Land Registry (UK):

    • If a borrower defaults, ConstructKoin repossesses and liquidates the property.
    • Capital and interest are repaid to stakers first, maximizing downside protection.
    • A portion of protocol fees also funds an insurance vault to cover extraordinary defaults.
    • Conservative 60-70% Loan-to-Value ratios provide substantial downside protection.
